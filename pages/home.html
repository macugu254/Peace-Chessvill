How a game works

Cards, not a boardEach card is a white piece on a square of a small board. A black king hides somewhere on it.
Announce a checkmateSay "checkmate in 2" and keep those cards face down. Everyone else gets one turn to spoil it.
Reveal and winPlace your cards one at a time. If the king is trapped, you win the game. Win 3 games to win the tournament.
