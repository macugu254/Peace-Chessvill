    How the cards work
    • Each card names a piece and a square, like Qf6 (white queen on f6). Files run h, g, f, e and ranks run 8, 7, 6, 5, so h8 is the corner. This is the board from Black's side.
    • King cards (K) are black. They are never placed. Casting one moves the black king to that square.
    • White pawns move toward rank 8 and capture diagonally forward. Pieces cannot jump, except knights.
    • Cards are placed one at a time. After each one the king answers. If it is in check it must escape. If not, it stays, unless it can capture an unprotected neighbour.
    • You win when the last card you place gives checkmate. Cards you do not need are overflow.
    • Swaps: a pawn card on rank 8 (Ph8, Pg8, Pf8, Pe8) can be swapped for a knight, bishop, rook, queen or king on the same square, taken from the spare deck. It is free, can be done several times in a turn, and is public. The pawn goes to the bottom of the main deck.
    • A card can exist twice, once in the main deck and once in the spare deck. You cannot place both on one square.
    • Two cards cannot share a square, and nothing can go on the king's square.
    • Each turn you may discard one card to the bottom of the deck and draw the face-up top card, or cast a king card and draw. Wrong mate? No penalty here, but the clock keeps running.
