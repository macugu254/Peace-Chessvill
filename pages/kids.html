Tutorial for kids

chessVill4

Can you catch a sneaky king? In chessVill-4 you play with cards on a tiny board. Let's learn it together, one step at a time. You can touch every board on this page.

1The tiny board

Chess is played on a big board with 64 squares. chessVill-4 uses only one small corner of it: 16 squares, 4 across and 4 down.

Every square has a name made of a letter and a number. The letters are the columns, called files: h, g, f, e. The numbers are the rows, called ranks: 8, 7, 6, 5.

We look at the board from the black king's side, so the corner at the top left is h8.

Try it: touch any square to hear its name.

2Meet the cards

There is no board in the real game. You play with cards. Every card has a piece and a square written on it.

♛Qh6
Q = Queenh = file h6 = rank 6

So Qh6 means "a Queen on square h6". The letters for the pieces are: P pawn, N knight, B bishop, R rook, Q queen and K king.

There are two kinds of cards:

White cardsYour team. These are pawns, knights, bishops, rooks and queens. You place them to trap the king.
King cards
♚Kf6
The black king is the one we are hunting. A king card tells you where the king is hiding. You can also play one to make the king jump to a new square.

3Your team: how the pieces see

Each piece "sees" some squares. If the black king stands on a square a piece can see, the king is in check. Pick a piece and touch the board. The yellow dots show every square it sees.

Pieces cannot see through other pieces. In the real game, cards you have already placed can block the way.

4Check and checkmate

Check means the king is being attacked. A king in check must get out of it. Checkmate means the king is attacked and has nowhere safe to go. That is how you win!

The black king never moves on its own. It only moves to answer your cards.

5What the king does

After each card you place, the king answers by these rules:

In check?
It must run to a safe square, or eat a card that has no guard.
Not in check?
It stays where it is. It may only eat an unguarded card next to it.
Guarded?
A card is guarded when one of your other cards sees its square. The king may never eat a guarded card.

6Your turn

Each player holds 4 cards. On your turn you do one of these:

Discard and drawPut one card at the bottom of the deck and take the top card. The top card is always face up, so you know what is coming.
Cast a king cardPlay a king card from your hand. The king jumps to that card's square, and you draw a new card.
Announce a checkmateSay "Checkmate in 2" (or 3 or 4) and keep that many cards. Next chapter!
PassDo nothing this turn.

Swap a pawn. A pawn on rank 8 (Ph8, Pg8, Pf8 or Pe8) is a bit weak. It can be swapped for a stronger piece on the same square, taken from the spare deck. Swapping is free, so you can do it as many times as you like on your turn.

7Announce and defend

This is the exciting part. You think you can trap the king, but there is a catch: everyone else gets a chance to ruin your plan. It happens in three steps.

Step 1: Announce

You say out loud: "Checkmate in 2!" (or 3, or 4). The number is how many cards you will place. You pick that many cards from your hand and keep them face down, so nobody knows which ones they are.

The other cardsAny cards you did not keep are discarded to the bottom of the deck. Your announcing turn ends.
Why a number?It is a clue. "In 2" tells the others you only need two cards, so your mate is short and sneaky.

You need at least 2 cards, and you cannot keep king cards. The king is the one you are hunting, so king cards never go in a mate.

Step 2: The others defend

Now every other player has one turn before you reveal. Nobody can see your cards, so defending is a clever guess. The players can:

Cast a king card (best!)The king jumps to a new square. Your mate was planned for the old square, so it may not work now. If several players cast king cards, the last one counts.
Do something elseThey can discard and draw, swap a pawn, pass, or even announce their own mate. But then they are not defending.

A defender with no king card in hand cannot move the king. Keeping a king card is like keeping a shield.

Try it: you are Leo. Mia announced "Checkmate in 2" and the king stands on h8. You hold four king cards. Which one do you play?

Step 3: Reveal

When your turn comes round again, you turn your cards over and place them one at a time, in any order you like. After each card, the king answers by the rules from chapter 5.

It worksIf checkmate happens, you win the game! If you reach it before your last card, you stop there.
It failsNo penalty. You draw cards back up to 4 and the game goes on. You learned where the king was, and so did everyone else.

You may swap a pawn from rank 8 for a stronger piece before you reveal. That can rescue a mate that a defender spoiled.

More things to know

Two announcers
If two players announce, each one reveals when the turn gets to them. The first real checkmate wins.
Hard squares
A king in a corner is easy to trap. A king in the middle (g7, f7, g6, f6) has lots of room, so it is the hardest to mate. Smart defenders play king cards on middle squares.
Smart announcers
Pick a mate that works on many squares. Then a defender's king card is less likely to spoil it.
Bluffing
You may announce without a real mate. If the others waste their king cards defending, that helps you. But you will draw cards and lose a turn when it fails.

8Clocks and winning

Clock
Each player has 15 minutes. The clock runs only on your own turn. If it hits zero, you are out.
Win a game
Be the first to reveal a real checkmate.
Win it all
Win 3 games to win the tournament.
Show board
The game has a Show board button. Keep it on while you learn. Switch it off when you want the big brain challenge.

10Cheat sheet

Card
Piece letter, then file, then rank. Qh6 = queen on h6.
Board
Files h g f e. Ranks 8 7 6 5. The corner is h8.
Check
The king is attacked.
Checkmate
The king is attacked and cannot escape or eat anything.
Guarded
One of your cards sees that square, so the king cannot eat it.
Turn
Discard and draw, cast a king card, announce, or pass.
Defend
Play a king card to move the king and spoil an announced mate.
